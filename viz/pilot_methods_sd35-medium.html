capVendi caption-Vendi, gridScore VLM rater and TPIPS are scored on each prompt's own human axis (no canonical-axis floor subtracted). DINO is DINOv2-Vendi, which is axis-blind — where it disagrees with capVendi is the point. TPIPS/o is the TPIPS share: the axis distance over the same four images' unconditioned distance, so it asks how much of a method's diversity landed on the axis rather than how much there was. It sits below 1 for every method (conditioning on one axis always leaves fewer ways to differ), so read it only against vanilla — a method can raise TPIPS while lowering TPIPS/o, which is diversity bought off-axis. Averaged geometrically, being a ratio. Small figure is the change vs vanilla, prompt-paired. Full tables and significance: evaluation/pilot_methods/RESULTS.md.
